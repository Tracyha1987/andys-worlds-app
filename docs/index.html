Privacy Policy

Andy's Worlds is a learning game for students in grades 4–10. We built it so that it collects no personal information.

  • No data collection. The app does not collect, store on a server, or share any personal information. There are no accounts, no sign-in, and no chat.
  • No ads, no tracking. The app contains no advertising and no analytics or tracking tools, and it does not use any third-party SDKs that collect data.
  • Progress stays on the device. Stars, levels, badges and quiz results are saved only on the device, inside the app. Deleting the app deletes this data. Parents can also clear it in the app under “Góc Ba Mẹ / Parent corner → Settings”.
  • Works offline. The main game works without internet and does not send your child’s answers anywhere.
  • Online play (optional): Tug of War and Rocket Race. If players choose to play online, their devices (2 to 4) connect directly to each other using a 5-letter room code, through the free PeerJS connection service. Only the nickname, avatar, grade and live game score are sent to the other player for that match. There is no chat, nothing is saved on a server, and as with any internet connection the connection service sees the devices’ IP addresses. We recommend children play online only with people they know.
  • Read-aloud. The “Hear it” buttons use the speech voice built into the device.
  • Purchases. The app is free to try for 7 days. After that, the full game can be unlocked with a one-time in-app purchase. Payment is handled entirely by Apple; we never see your payment details. There are no subscriptions. Stars earned in the game are play points only and cannot be bought with real money.

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
